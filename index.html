What we build

Three ways companies usually need us. All of them start the same way: a conversation about the problem, not the solution.

Websites

Marketing sites, product pages, shops. Designed around how your customers actually move, not around page counts. Fast, legible, and yours to host anywhere.

Web applications

Portals, dashboards, customer areas. Software that does something when you click it, backed by data that belongs to you — with an admin side your team can use without asking us first.

Standalone programs

Automations, internal tools, one-off utilities. If a task repeats itself every week, it probably shouldn't be manual. We'll tell you straight if it needs software at all.

How a build runs

Five steps. No black box, no disappearing acts, no big-reveal on the last day.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A short call about what's broken or missing. You get a fixed quote within five working days — a number, not a range with asterisks.

  2. 02

    Design

    You see a clickable prototype before any final code exists. Changes are cheap now and expensive later, so we spend the time here.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos while we work. You watch the thing take shape instead of waiting for a demo nobody prepared for.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We ship, monitor, and stay on the line for two weeks. No "good luck, it's Friday" deploys.

  5. 05

    Support

    Monthly care or ad-hoc, your call. Same team that built it, so nothing gets lost in a handover.
